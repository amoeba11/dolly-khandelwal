I studied computer science at BITS Pilani in India, then spent three years at Adobe working on search and personalization — matching users to the right content to keep them engaged.

To broaden my expertise, I moved to Microsoft to explore enterprise cloud and AI, where I led the org's first Gen AI product from scratch: building it, shaping go-to-market strategy, and shipping monthly releases driven by customer feedback.

Along the way, my interest shifted from how to build to what to build and why — which is what led me to my MBA. I'm most energized by the business questions at the intersection of tech: how tech creates value, and how it captures that value at scale.
